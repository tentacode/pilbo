Pilbo Français

Your assistant for taking your medications

Pilbo is a virtual pill box designed for people who take a lot of medications and sometimes forget a dose. It is free, with no account, and your data stays on your phone.

For iPhone and Android, in English and French.

What Pilbo does

  • Pill box: your intakes at different times, on the days you choose, with customized medications to recognize them at a glance.
  • Reminders: a discreet notification every 10 minutes, to confirm from the notification or the app, with the sound of your choice.
  • Calendar: the history of your intakes.
  • Stocks: each intake lowers your stock, and a notification alerts you before you run out.
  • Privacy: free, no ads, no account and no server. Pilbo is open source.
  • Accessibility: designed to meet WCAG / RAAM, works with screen readers.
