Pilbo is a virtual pill box designed for people who take a lot of medications and sometimes forget a dose. Pilbo is completely free, with no account, and your data stays on your phone.

YOUR MEDICATION INTAKES IN YOUR PILL BOX
• Organize your intakes at different times, each with its medications and quantities.
• Pick the days of the week that suit you.
• Customize the shape, size and colors of your medications to recognize them at a glance.

DISCREET, EFFECTIVE REMINDERS
• A discreet notification every 10 minutes, instead of an alarm you turn off without taking your medications.
• Confirm an intake right from the notification or in the app.
• Customize the sound of your reminders.
• Find the history of your intakes in a calendar.

NO MORE EMPTY BOXES
• Choose to track the stock of the important medications in your pill box.
• Each intake automatically lowers your stock.
• A notification alerts you before you run out.
• Add a new box to your stock in one tap.
• It also works for your everyday medications (e.g. paracetamol).

PRIVACY
• Pilbo is free, with no ads and no collection of your data.
• No account, no server: your data is only on your phone.
• Pilbo works without an internet connection.
• Pilbo is an open source project: https://github.com/tentacode/pilbo

ACCESSIBILITY
• Pilbo was designed to meet the WCAG / RAAM standards.
• The app works with assistive technologies: screen readers, keyboard navigation…

Available in English and French.

Pilbo is not a medical device. It does not replace the advice of a doctor or pharmacist.
